What helps in a first message

None of this is required, but the more of it you can share up front, the more useful the first conversation will be.

  • The problem — what is not working, or what you are trying to stand up.
  • The estate — roughly where you are today: greenfield, an established landing zone, a migration in flight, a tenant consolidation.
  • The constraint — regulatory obligations, security classification, an audit finding, a deadline someone else set.
  • The shape of the help — a design deliverable, a review, or an architect embedded with your team for a period.

Working together

Engagements are contracted through SGKeystone Ltd on a statement of work with defined deliverables, milestones and acceptance criteria. We can work through your preferred supplier or procurement route, and are experienced with the security clearance and assurance requirements that come with regulated and government-adjacent environments.

Enquiries are usually answered within one working day. If a piece of work is not a good fit for us, we will say so early — and where we can, point you towards someone better placed.
