Platform and network

Landing zone design for new estates and for estates that have already grown past the model they started with. The typical output is a high-level and low-level design covering management group hierarchy, subscription vending, hub-and-spoke or virtual WAN topology, and the policy set that keeps the structure from drifting.

  • Landing zones — CAF-aligned structure adapted to how the organisation actually operates, including sandbox, connectivity, identity and workload subscriptions.
  • Network topology — hub-and-spoke and virtual WAN, Azure Virtual Network Manager, Network Security Perimeter, ExpressRoute, private connectivity and DNS, layer 4 and layer 7 load balancing.
  • Governance — Azure Policy, tagging and naming, privileged access paths, and the operating model for change so the design survives the first year.

Identity and access

Entra ID architecture treated as platform, not as a collection of portal clicks. The work covers how people, guests and non-human identities are created, authorised, reviewed and retired, and how that model is expressed in Conditional Access, RBAC and entitlement management.

  • Directory architecture — tenant design, administrative unit strategy, hybrid considerations, and the boundary between human and workload identity.
  • Lifecycle — joiner-mover-leaver automation, access packages, access reviews, and named ownership for service principals and managed identities.
  • Control plane — Conditional Access rationalisation, privileged access, RBAC and ABAC models, external identities, and least privilege that can actually be operated.

Data, analytics and AI

Data platforms landed with the same private networking, identity and governance expectations as the rest of the estate. Microsoft Foundry and analytics services are treated as workloads that inherit the landing zone, not as exceptions that bypass it.

  • Data platforms — Azure SQL and managed instances, analytics estates, storage tiers including Azure NetApp Files, backup and access models that can be explained to an auditor.
  • Foundry and AI landing — private networking, managed identity, model and data governance, and a wrapper that makes the deployment operable rather than experimental.
  • Migration and consolidation — SQL estate reduction, Private Link and DNS re-pointing, and cut-over sequences with a rollback position.

Containers and workspaces

Container platforms and virtual desktop estates designed for the constraints the organisation actually has — including hardened baselines, air-gapped operation, and an operating model that does not assume outbound internet for day-to-day support.

  • Kubernetes and containers — cluster and platform design, registry and supply chain controls, image promotion between enclaves, and service operations for segregated environments.
  • Hardened and air-gapped — no outbound connectivity, offline registry paths, classification boundaries, and runbooks that cleared staff can follow without escalating to the public cloud console.
  • Virtual desktop — Azure Virtual Desktop delivered through Nerdio, with identity, network and image management aligned to the rest of the platform.

Design, documentation and assurance

The documents are the product as much as the diagrams. High-level and low-level designs, architecture decision records with the rejected options written down, and a build path that delivery teams can execute without reconstructing intent from a workshop recording.

  • Design packs — HLD and LLD to a standard that engineers and assurance functions can both use, including network, identity, policy and operations.
  • Decision records — trade-offs captured at the time they were made, including where a legacy dependency forces a compromise against Zero Trust or a reference framework.
  • Audit-ready writing — language and structure that survive review by security, risk and audit, without substituting framework recitation for the actual design.

How we engage

Work is contracted through SGKeystone Ltd on a statement of work with defined deliverables, milestones and acceptance criteria. Typical shapes:

Design review or assessment

A short engagement against an existing estate or a proposed design. Findings, risks and a recommended path, written so they can be acted on rather than filed.

Architecture lead

Embedded alongside platform, security and delivery teams for a defined period. Produces the design and stays through enough of the build to keep it honest.

Platform design and build

Multi-month landing zone, identity or container platform work, from target architecture through implementation support and handover.

Design for others to build

A complete design pack and decision record handed to your delivery organisation or a third party, with optional review points during build.

We can work through your preferred supplier or procurement route, including the clearance and assurance expectations that come with regulated and government-adjacent environments.
